# Company Travel Policy (Demo Document)

> This is synthetic demo content seeded by AgentOS. It does not describe a real company.

## Flights

Employees may book economy class for flights under six hours. Business class is
permitted only for flights longer than six hours and requires VP approval in
advance. Upgrades using personal funds or miles are always allowed.

## Accommodation

Hotel bookings should not exceed 220 USD per night in standard locations and
320 USD per night in high-cost cities (New York, London, Zurich, Tokyo,
San Francisco). Exceptions require manager approval before booking.

## Expenses

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of travel completion.
Receipts are required for every expense above 25 USD. Per-diem for meals is
65 USD per travel day; alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Remote work while travelling

Working remotely from another country for more than 14 consecutive days
requires HR approval due to tax implications.
