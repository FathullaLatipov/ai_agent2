## How we investigate underperforming products

When a product's revenue share falls materially below the portfolio average,
review the following common causes in order:

1. **Pricing drift** — check whether the street price has moved against
   comparable competitor items in the last two quarters. Price increases above
   8% without a feature change historically reduce units by 15–25%.
2. **Promotion coverage** — products excluded from the seasonal campaign
   calendar typically lose 10–18% of expected revenue. Verify the product is in
   at least one active campaign.
3. **Inventory availability** — stockouts longer than 3 days per month depress
   the ranking signal in the storefront for up to six weeks.
4. **Catalog quality** — listings with fewer than 4 photos or missing dimension
   tables convert roughly 30% worse.
5. **Lifecycle stage** — items beyond 24 months since launch usually need a
   refresh or managed end-of-life rather than additional promotion.

## Standard remediation actions

- Re-price within ±5% of the category median and monitor for two weeks.
- Add the product to the next campaign slot with a 10% introductory discount.
- If lifecycle-driven, plan a stock run-down and a successor launch brief.

## Escalation

If revenue share does not recover within one quarter, escalate to the category
manager with the measured metrics and the actions already taken.
